- 2026-11-24: 1.0.0 build 3, from Brian's test of build 2 (branch claude/build3-crash-swipe-haptic).
  - Crash (the blocker). Taking a punch list item's photo: the punch list's camera button sends the app back to the camera, and a quick shutter press then called expo-camera while its capture session was still stopped. expo-camera's takePicture (CameraPhotoCapture.swift) calls AVCapturePhotoOutput.capturePhoto without checking the session is running; on a stopped session iOS raises an Objective-C exception ("No active and enabled video connection") that JavaScript cannot catch, so the app dies. Our `ready` flag could not prevent it: the camera view stayed mounted under every other screen with `active` off (session stopped), and onCameraReady fires only once per native view, so `ready` stayed true from the first launch of the camera. Fix: the camera view is mounted only while the camera is on screen (a fresh view fires onCameraReady after its session is running), `ready` drops the moment the camera is covered, and a press while not ready waits for ready (up to 3 seconds, then says "The camera was not ready. Take it again.") instead of calling the camera. Captures also run one at a time, since expo-camera rejects a second capture while one is in flight, which build 2 showed as an alert during quick shots. The punch list, the keyboard and the pending After were checked: none of them crashes by itself; they only made the return to the camera and the press quicker than the session restart.
  - Also on that path: a photo for a punch list item no longer becomes a Before because the Before switch was left on earlier (the switch is hidden in that mode).
  - Swipe between photos: opened from a job's Photos grid, the photo screen swipes left and right through the job's photos in the grid's order (newest day first, each day in the order taken); each page keeps its own date, place, caption, tags and actions, and the header says "3 of 15". From a pair or a punch list item it still shows the one photo.
  - Shutter haptic: the haptic is now the first thing every press does, with no early return before it (build 2 skipped it when the camera was not ready yet, the likely cause of the missing first one). Stamping, which holds the JavaScript thread for a moment per 12 MP photo, now waits until no capture is in flight and the shutter has been still for 1.2 seconds, so presses and haptics in a burst are not held up behind it.
  - Location for quick shots and stale fixes (Brian's train photo with no location): build 2 kept the last reading and dropped it only after 2 minutes, and its watch could stall: CoreLocation pauses updates on its own when it thinks the phone is stationary (expo-location leaves that on), a paused watch does not resume by itself, and the 3 m distance filter sent nothing while standing still. On a train stopped at a station and moving off, the watch went quiet and the reading aged past 2 minutes, so that photo got no place. Now: the watch uses no distance filter (readings keep coming, about once a second), a watchdog restarts the watch when 10 seconds pass with no reading, the shutter reads the place at the press itself, and a reading is reused only while it still says where the phone is: at most 30 seconds old, no more than 50 m of possible movement at the speed the phone reported with it (on a train at 30 m/s that is under 2 seconds), and at most 10 seconds with no speed reported. Anything older prints no place; the app never prints a place the phone did not report for that moment. The photo screen now says "Location was off, or the phone had no recent fix".
  - app.json `ios.buildNumber` 3; version stays 1.0.0. CLAUDE.md has the two new rules.
  - Checked: `npx tsc --noEmit` clean, `npm test` 73 passing (new tests/camera.test.mjs: when the camera may be called, the camera view mounted only while on screen, presses wait for ready and run one at a time, the item photo role, the haptic first in every press, when stamping may start, the fix age and drift rule including the train, the quiet watch, the swipe order), `npx expo export --platform ios` bundles. The crash, the haptic and the location timing need a device: TestFlight steps below.

## TestFlight checklist (1.0.0 build 1), around a real job

Before the build: nothing left to fill in (the Expo project id and the icon are done).

1. Fresh install. Go through the three screens, allow the camera, deny location the first time, skip Photos saving. Close the paywall after its X appears (3 seconds); the "Free, for good" sheet shows.
2. Make a job from the job chip: "New job" is free the first time. Name it after the real job, fill the client, use the spot's address.
3. Take five photos with location denied: the stamp shows the date and time only, the "Location off" pill shows, the shutter fires at once and "Saved to <job>" appears. Open the job: five photos under Today.
4. Turn location on (the pill's sheet, or Settings). Take more photos: the address appears in the live stamp within a few seconds. Turn on Airplane Mode and take one: the coordinates print instead of the address. Turn the phone sideways and take one: the photo is landscape and the stamp sits in the same corner as the preview showed.
5. Before and after (Pro, or after starting the trial): switch to Before, shoot a spot. Open that photo, Take the After: the Before shows faintly; line up and shoot. Open the job's Before and after tab, open the pair, Share the pair (one side-by-side image).
6. Punch list: add two items, attach a photo to one with its camera button, tick one off.
7. Build report: check the cover, the photos by day with captions, the pair side by side, the punch list with Done and Open, then Client signs here and sign. Share report to Files and open the PDF; check its size and that the stamps are readable. Print to a printer or Save as PDF.
8. Stamp editor: try each template, corner and size, 24 hour with seconds, a note; check the live stamp matches the saved photo. Open a photo, Fix the stamp, change the note; the photo updates and the time and place stay.
9. Settings: add a company name and a logo (PNG with a clear background, and a HEIC photo); with Pro both appear on the stamp. Turn on Save copies to Photos: iOS asks for add-only access; take a photo and find it in Photos.
10. Paywalls: as a free user (Settings, seven taps on the version line, Act as a free user), add a second job (paywall), build a report for a second job (paywall), tap Before (paywall), Import from Photos (paywall). Restore purchases on each, and on a second device with the same Apple ID.
11. Rating prompt: after a report is shared, at most once; never over the camera.
12. Dark mode on the phone: the app stays light outside the camera, as designed. Largest Dynamic Type size and VoiceOver on the camera controls, the job chip and the shutter.

## Noticed, not changed

- Build 4 TestFlight steps: tap Before, take two photos: only the first is a Before and the switch shows Photo again. In Job 1's Before and after tab, take the mark off the unpaired Befores. Import an iPhone screenshot: its stamp reads like a camera photo's. Build the report: coordinates under photos stamped without an address. Still to retry from build 3: the punch list camera.
- Report layout: portrait photos sit two to a row, so 34 portrait photos take about 10 pages. Three to a row for portrait photos is described in the build 4 pull request, not built.
- The Before chip sits just above the shutter. With Before now one-shot, an accidental tap costs one photo's mark at most, but a little more space between the chips and the shutter would help gloved hands.
- Build 3 TestFlight steps: (1) In a job's Punch list with the keyboard up, tick an item done, tap its camera button and press the shutter at once, several times; then take a Before, its After, and go straight to a punch list photo. No crash; a press before the camera shows waits and takes the photo. (2) Open a photo from the grid and swipe through all of them; edit a caption on one, swipe away and back. (3) Take five photos fast: a haptic on each; then the "Saved to" lines. (4) On a moving vehicle and walking: every photo has a place, and Airplane Mode or a tunnel gives none rather than an old one.
- Coming back to the camera now restarts the camera view, so the preview is black for a moment (about half a second) after closing a job or Settings. This is the price of never calling a stopped camera; it also frees the camera while the person is in other screens.
- In a fast burst, each press is captured right after the one before it finishes (expo-camera takes one picture at a time), so a fifth press within a second is captured a little after the press; its stamp shows the press time. The stamping of a burst starts 1.2 seconds after the last press, and the "Saved to" lines come after that.
- expo-haptics makes a new feedback generator for each impact and prepares it right before firing, so the very first haptic after a long idle can still be faint on some iPhones; there is no way to prepare it ahead from JavaScript.
- Reverse geocoding on a fast train: the address is reused only within 60 m of where it was looked up, and Apple throttles lookups, so on a train many photos print coordinates instead of an address. That is honest; a faster lookup is not possible without sending more requests to Apple.
- Purpose strings Apple could ask for later, not added (build 1 passed processing without them): NSMicrophoneUsageDescription (expo-camera links audio capture for video; the app never records audio) and NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription (expo-image-picker, expo-media-library and expo-file-system link library-read APIs; the app only adds photos and uses the system picker). If Apple flags either, add an honest string for it.
- The burn runs on the JS thread with native Skia. In WebAssembly it takes about 0.8 s for 12 MP; on a phone it should be quicker, but the UI may pause briefly while a photo is saved. If it shows on device, the next step is moving the burn to a worklet thread.
- The map thumbnail (Pro, off unless turned on) uses react-native-maps' snapshot of Apple Maps, which needs signal and loads map tiles from Apple for that area. It is disclosed beside the switch in the stamp editor, in the privacy policy and here; the photos stay on the phone. Only a device can show how quickly the snapshot comes back.
- An imported photo with no date in its file prints no date or time, and its report caption says "Imported" in place of a time. Its record keeps the import time for sorting by day.
- Fix the stamp does not redraw the map; a photo whose stamp had a map loses it when the stamp is fixed.
- The report's photo copies are made one by one on the JS thread; a 50-photo report takes a few seconds to build on screen ("Building the report").
- The renders' sample photos are drawn scenes, which compress far better than real photos; the 30-photo test report is 0.6 MB, a real one will be larger. The size estimate in src/report/plan.ts assumes busy photos (0.35 bytes a pixel, about 4 MB for 30) and stays under 10 MB.
- Estimate Maker's notification handler and trial reminder are kept; the reminder asks for notification permission on the onboarding reminder screen only when a trial is on offer.
- The app is light only outside the camera (`userInterfaceStyle: light`), as briefed.
