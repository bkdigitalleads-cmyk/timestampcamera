## Description

Timestamp Camera is the camera for people who photograph their work. Every photo shows when and where it was taken, every photo belongs to a job, and the day ends with a photo report your client can read.

DATE, TIME AND PLACE ON EVERY PHOTO
- The stamp is printed into the photo at full resolution: date, time, street address, latitude and longitude, altitude, a note and the job name.
- Compact, Standard or Full, in any corner, in three sizes. 12 or 24 hour clock, seconds if you want them.
- The live stamp shows on the camera exactly where it will print.
- Your company name and logo on the stamp, and a small map of the spot, with Pro.

PHOTOS FILED BY JOB
- Pick the job once on the camera; every shot lands there, by day.
- Save copies to your Photos library too, if you like.
- The original of every photo stays in the app, so a stamp can be fixed later.

BEFORE AND AFTER
- Mark a photo Before, then take its After from the same spot with the Before shown faintly to line up the shot.
- Share the pair as one side-by-side image.

PUNCH LIST
- Keep what is left to do inside the job, attach photos, tick items off as the work gets done.

A PHOTO REPORT YOUR CLIENT CAN READ
- Your company and logo, the job, the client and the dates, then the photos in a grid with captions, before and after pairs side by side, the punch list with each item's status, and the client's signature.
- Share it by Mail or Messages, save it to Files, or print it.

WORKS WITHOUT SIGNAL
- No signal in the basement? The stamp prints the coordinates instead of the address. The shutter never waits.
- Location off? The stamp shows the date and time.

NOTHING LEAVES THE PHONE
- No account, no server. Photos, locations and reports stay on your iPhone until you share them.
- Location is used only while the app is open.

Built for contractors, handymen, cleaners, landscapers, inspectors, property managers and field crews who send proof of the work the same day.

Free: the camera, every stamp line, one job, saving to Photos and your first photo report, with no watermark.
Timestamp Camera Pro: unlimited jobs and reports, before and after pairs, punch lists, your company name, logo and a map on the stamp, and stamping photos you already took. Weekly with a 3-day free trial, yearly, or once for life. Payment is charged to your Apple ID; subscriptions renew until cancelled in your Apple ID settings at least 24 hours before the period ends.

The stamp prints your iPhone's own clock and location at the moment you take the photo.

## App Review note

Timestamp Camera is a camera for job photos. It prints the date, time and place on each photo, files photos by job, pairs before and after photos, keeps a punch list, and builds a PDF photo report. There is no account and no server; nothing is uploaded.

Free: the camera, every stamp line, all templates, one job, saving copies to Photos, fixing a stamp, and the first photo report. Free photos carry no extra watermark.
Pro (weekly with a 3-day free trial, yearly, lifetime): more jobs, company name and logo on the stamp, the map thumbnail, before and after pairs, punch lists, photo reports after the first, and importing existing photos.

The onboarding paywall appears after three short screens and the permission screens; its close button (X) appears after 3 seconds. The in-app paywall opens when adding a second job, building a second report, or tapping a locked feature (Before, punch list, company or logo on the stamp, import), and from Settings. Restore purchases is on every paywall and in Settings.

Permissions, each asked with its reason on its own screen and each optional:
- Camera: to take the job photos.
- Location, while using the app only (never in the background): to print where each photo was taken. With location denied, the app still works and the stamp shows the date and time only.
- Add to Photos (add-only): asked only when the person turns on saving copies to Photos.

How to test: open the app, take a photo (it goes to Job 1), tap the thumbnail at the bottom left to open the job, tap Build report and Share report. Tap the job chip at the top of the camera and New job to see the in-app paywall. Settings, Restore purchases works with a sandbox account.
