How it works

The stamp. Tap Stamp next to the shutter to choose Compact (one line), Standard (three lines) or Full (every line you turned on), the corner and the size, and switch each line on or off: date, time (12 or 24 hour, with seconds if you like), address, coordinates, altitude, the job name and a note. Your company name, logo and a small map of the spot are part of Pro.

Jobs. The chip at the top of the camera says which job the next photo goes to. Tap it to switch jobs or add one. Each job keeps its photos by day.

Before and after. Switch the camera to Before, or open a photo and mark it Before. Then open it again and tap Take the After: the Before shows faintly over the camera so you can line up the same shot.

Punch list. Inside a job, add what is left to do, attach photos with the camera button, and tick items off.

Photo report. In a job, tap Build report. Choose the days and what goes in, have the client sign on the phone if they are there, then Share (Mail, Messages, Save to Files) or Print.

Correcting a stamp. The app keeps the original of every photo. Open a photo and tap Fix the stamp to change the note, the template or the corner; the date, time and place stay as recorded.

Questions

Does it work without signal?
Yes. Photos save with the date, time and coordinates. The street address needs signal; without it the coordinates print in its place.
What if I turn location off?
The app still works. The stamp then shows the date and time only.
Is the full photo stamped, or just a preview?
The stamp is burned into the saved photo at full resolution. The original, without the stamp, stays inside the app so a stamp can be fixed.
Where are my photos?
Inside the app, filed by job. Turn on Save copies to Photos in Settings to also add each stamped photo to your Photos library.
Can I stamp photos I already took?
With Pro: in a job, tap Import from Photos. The stamp uses the photo's own date and place when the file has them, and the photo is marked Imported in the app.
What does the stamp prove?
It prints your iPhone's clock and location at the moment you took the photo, nothing more.
What is free and what is Pro?
Free forever: the camera, every stamp line, one job, saving to Photos and your first photo report, with no watermark. Pro adds more jobs, your company name and logo on the stamp, the map, before and after pairs, punch lists, more reports and importing photos. Weekly with a 3-day free trial, yearly, or once for life; prices are shown in the app.
How do I restore Pro on a new iPhone?
Settings, Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.
How do I cancel?
In your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before the trial or period ends.
Is anything uploaded?
Not to us: there is no account and no server of ours, and photos and reports stay on your iPhone until you share them. The street address on the stamp is looked up by Apple's location service, which receives the coordinates for that; turn the Address line off in Stamp and no lookup is made. The optional map picture loads from Apple Maps. See the privacy policy.
