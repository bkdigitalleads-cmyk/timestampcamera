Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

What the app collects

Purchases. If you buy Timestamp Camera Pro, Apple processes the payment, and RevenueCat, the service the app uses to confirm Pro, keeps your purchase history for this app under a random anonymous identifier. It is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

That is all. It is the only item on the app's App Store privacy label: Purchases, not linked to you, not used for tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your photos, the originals kept for correcting a stamp, your jobs, punch lists, signatures, company name, logo and reports are stored only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server of ours, and we receive none of it. A photo or report leaves your phone only when you share it, print it or save a copy to Photos.

What goes to Apple

Two features use Apple's services, and Apple receives your coordinates for them:

The address on the stamp. To print a street address, the app asks Apple's location service to look up the coordinates where you are standing (reverse geocoding). Apple receives those coordinates for that lookup. It happens only while the camera or the stamp settings are open and the stamp's Address line is on. Turn the Address line off and no lookup is made; the stamp can still print the coordinates, which come from your iPhone itself.

The optional map picture. If you turn on the map on the stamp (Pro, off unless you turn it on), the small map picture loads from Apple Maps, so Apple receives the area being shown.

Apple handles this under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive these coordinates or the results.

Camera, location and Photos

The camera takes your job photos. Location is used only while the app is open, never in the background, to print where each photo was taken; for the street address, see What goes to Apple above. Photos access is add-only and asked for only if you turn on saving copies to Photos. The app works with any of these turned off.
