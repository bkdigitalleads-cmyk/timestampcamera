Timestamp Camera

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

What the app collects

Purchases. If you buy Timestamp Camera Pro, Apple processes the payment, and RevenueCat, the service the app uses to confirm Pro, keeps your purchase history for this app under a random anonymous identifier. It is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

That is all. It is the only item on the app's App Store privacy label: Purchases, not linked to you, not used for tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your photos, the originals kept for correcting a stamp, the places they were taken, your jobs, punch lists, signatures, company name, logo and reports are stored only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server, and nothing is uploaded. A photo or report leaves your phone only when you share it, print it or save a copy to Photos.

Camera, location and Photos

The camera takes your job photos. Location is used only while the app is open, never in the background, to print where each photo was taken; the address comes from your iPhone's own geocoder, which asks Apple for the street name of those coordinates, as the Maps app does. Photos access is add-only and asked for only if you turn on saving copies to Photos. The app works with any of these turned off.

If you turn on the map on the stamp (off unless you turn it on), the small map picture comes from Apple Maps, which loads the map for the area shown, like any map in any app. Your photos never leave the phone for it.
